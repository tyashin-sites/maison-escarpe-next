# Maison Escarpe — Brand brief (research-derived, 2026-09-27)

> Proposed by Tyashin/Claude from a competitor teardown (15 brands across prestige niche, oud/attar
> specialists and DTC winners) and a naming + visual-identity study. The customer supplied only:
> Burlington, Ontario launch → US expansion; oil-based oud/attar perfumes at **40% oil**; "shake well
> before use"; long-lasting; positioned above LV / D&G / Armani on exclusivity; no marketing budget.
> Everything below is a **recommendation awaiting sign-off**. Full reports: the agency's research
> files `research-competitors.md` and `research-brand.md` (handed over separately).

## Name — MAISON ESCARPE
- *Escarpe* (French): the steep inner wall of a fortress ditch; root of "escarpment". The **Niagara Escarpment**
  — 400-million-year-old dolostone, a UNESCO biosphere since 1990 — stands directly behind Burlington.
- Why it wins: true to place (the one thing no Gulf or Stockholm house can copy); bilingual by birth; a wall
  = an invitation-only house; hard consonants against a soft resinous product.
- Clearance (desk-level, 2026-09-27): no fragrance or cosmetics house named Escarpe / Maison Escarpe on
  Fragrantica, Parfumo, Instagram or general search. Only "Escarpe" in commerce: an Italian footwear
  e-tailer (escarpe.it, Class 25) and a Quebec climbing gym (L'Escarpé). **maisonescarpe.com and
  maisonescarpe.ca were both unregistered** (whois). escarpe.com is parked for sale; escarpe.ca is
  registered to a third party since 2017. Formal CIPO + USPTO Class 3 clearance is still required.
- Backup (also both domains free on 2026-09-27): **AURUMBRE** (aurum + umbra, "gold in shadow"; zero hits
  anywhere) — used here as the Private Blend vintage attar.
- Rejected after checks: Athar (four existing houses), Baqa (Ajmal product), Sarmad (Arabian Oud product),
  Shamal (Finnish brand on the same concept), Solenne, Oudera, Tola, Tamarack, Umbra, Tenebrae, Resina.

## Tagline & manifesto
- **Forty percent. Four hundred million years.**
- *We do not make perfume that announces itself. We make oil that stays — resin a tree spent decades
  defending, worn a hand's breadth from the heart, revealed only to those who come close.*

## Positioning (from the competitor teardown)
- The per-ml landscape: designer-prestige oud sprays $3.5–6/ml; niche sprays $5–8/ml; Amouage attars ~$49/ml
  (12 ml, $540–590); artisan pure-oud houses $180–2,000+/g. **Nothing sits between Amouage and the artisan
  tier with Western luxury presentation, online-only.** That is the slot.
- **dawam.ca** already sells oil-based "inspired by designer" scents in Canada at ~C$35 — the exact opposite
  position. Maison Escarpe must never read as a dupe brand: style homages are described, never named.
- No prestige house prints hours. Use a wear scale (Intimate / Present / Commanding) + design-intent
  language; keep any number for a logged wear test (Competition Bureau / FTC substantiation).
- Say "shake gently" as a ritual and a proof of naturalness (no solubilisers, no stabilisers), shown in
  macro imagery, paired with "hand-filled by weight".

## Zero-budget playbook (in order)
1. Founder-authored long-form on the domain: "why 40%", "how to wear attar oil", "what oud costs" (Journal).
2. Ceremonial critic seeding: numbered sample sets with a personal note to long-form reviewers
   (Kafkaesque, Cafleurebon, Basenotes/Fragrantica columnists, Ouddict), FTC-compliant disclosure.
3. Numbered batches with visible drops: timed pre-order to the list, sets before singles, sold-out archive kept online.
4. Paid discovery set with full credit (the set funds sampling).
5. Waitlist before inventory ("the list"); release batch one to the list first; email is the drop channel.
6. Fragrantica / Parfumo / Basenotes listings early. r/fragrance 90/10 participation. Fragtok micro-creators.
7. One collaboration with an established artisan house per year.
8. Macro video of the oil itself (viscosity, colour, the swirl) — content only an oil brand can make.

## Visual direction — "Dolostone & Dusk" (see DESIGN-SPEC.md)
Brown-black ink, wet-stone ground, unlacquered brass, dark oud garnet, limestone-cream paper; Bodoni Moda +
Manrope; tungsten raking light on real stone, smoked glass, walnut, raw agarwood. Sits outside both the
Gulf black-gold-crest template and the Byredo/Le Labo white-sans-cylinder template.

## Regulatory checklist (not legal advice — ASSET-DEBT #14)
Canada: Cosmetic Notification within 10 days of first sale; INCI list on the outer label; bilingual identity,
net quantity, warnings; fragrance-allergen disclosure (SOR/2024-63, phased 2026–2028); CITES paperwork
for *Aquilaria* oil; "Made in Canada" only with ≥51% Canadian direct cost + qualifier.
US: MoCRA registration/listing unless < US$1M US sales; safety substantiation mandatory; US adverse-event
contact on the label; net quantity in fl oz + mL; allergen rule pending.
Shipping: alcohol-free oil is outside hazmat Class 3 **only** if the finished blend's flash point clears 60 °C —
commission a closed-cup test per formula. US de minimis ended 2025-08-29: ship US by courier, DDP, with
CUSMA paperwork.
IFRA: Category 4 limits apply to the finished 40% product — about 8× less headroom than an EDT; obtain a
conformity certificate per formula.
